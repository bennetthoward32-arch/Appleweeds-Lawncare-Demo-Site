Services

Don’t see your job listed? Ask us. We take on a lot of small jobs around the yard.

  • Mowing and edging

    Weekly or biweekly mowing, trimmed edges along walks and beds, and clippings blown clean.

  • Hedge and shrub trimming

    Hedges and shrubs shaped and cut back so they stay healthy and off your siding, windows and walkways.

  • Mulch and pine straw

    Fresh mulch or pine straw to finish your beds, hold in moisture and keep the weeds down.

  • Bed cleanup and planting

    Weeding and clearing old growth, then new plants chosen for your sun and soil.

  • Leaf and seasonal cleanups

    Leaves removed in fall and a full reset in spring, with the debris hauled away.

  • Sod and lawn repair

    Bare spots patched, thin areas reseeded and new sod laid where you need it.

  • Wood fence installation and repair

    New wood fences, plus repairs for leaning posts, broken pickets and storm damage.

  • Handyman help

    Small repairs around the house and yard when you need an extra pair of hands.
